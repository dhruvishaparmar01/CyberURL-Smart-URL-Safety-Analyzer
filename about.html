About

CyberURL is a web-based cybersecurity awareness platform designed to help users identify potentially unsafe websites before interacting with them. With the increasing number of phishing attacks, fake websites, and online scams, many users find it difficult to determine whether a URL is trustworthy. CyberURL provides an easy-to-use interface where users can analyze website links, understand potential security risks, and learn how to recognize common cyber threats. Along with URL analysis, the platform also includes educational resources that promote safe browsing habits and improve cybersecurity awareness for everyone.
