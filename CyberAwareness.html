Cyber Awareness

Stay One Step Ahead: Recognizing Today's Most Advanced Cyber Scams

🤖 AI-Powered Email Phishing

📌 How it Works

AI creates highly realistic, personalized fake emails with phishing links to steal your data.

🛡️ Recommendations

  • Check the sender's exact email address carefully.
  • Avoid clicking unknown or unexpected links.
  • Visit official websites directly via your browser.

🎙️ AI Voice Cloning Scam

📌 How it Works

Attackers use AI to copy someone's voice and pretend to be a family member or friend. They usually create an emergency story and ask for money.

🛡️ Recommendations

  • Stay calm and verify the call.
  • Call the person on their real number.
  • Never send money without confirmation.

📱 QR Code Phishing (Quishing)

📌 How it Works

Fake QR codes redirect users to phishing websites that steal passwords or payment details. These QR codes may appear on posters, menus, or payment stands.

🛡️ Recommendations

  • Scan QR codes only from trusted sources.
  • Check the website before entering any details.
  • Avoid making payments on suspicious websites.

🏛️ Fake Banking Website

📌 How it Works

Scammers send messages claiming your bank account needs verification. The link opens a fake banking website that looks real and steals your login details.

🛡️ Recommendations

  • Never log in through message links.
  • Type your bank's website manually.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.

📷 Social Media Account Recovery Scam

📌 How it Works

Scammers promise to recover hacked social media accounts and ask for your OTP or password. Once shared, they take control of your account.

🛡️ Recommendations

  • Never share your OTP or password.
  • Use only the platform's official support.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.
